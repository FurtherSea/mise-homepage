iCloud sync

Mise syncs through your own private iCloud. If something doesn’t show up on another device:

  1. 1Open Settings in Mise, the gear on the Meal Plan page, and check iCloud sync says it’s connected.
  2. 2Make sure iCloud is on and the same Apple Account is signed in on each device.
  3. 3Give it a moment, then close and reopen Mise.

Getting started

  1. 1Add an ingredient to your kitchen.
  2. 2Create a recipe and put the ingredient in it.
  3. 3Add the dish to a meal plan.
  4. 4Open Shopping to see the list.

Data and privacy

Your ingredients, recipes, plans and photos stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Starting with version 2.0, the app also sends usage statistics to Google Analytics; the privacy policy has the details.
