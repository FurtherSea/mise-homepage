iCloud sync

Your kitchen, recipes and plans sync through your own private iCloud.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start.

Light and dark

Supports light and dark mode.

English and 中文

Switch the app’s language in Settings at any time.

Your kitchen data
stays yours

Your ingredients, recipes, plans and photos stay on your iPhone and, when iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud. Shiso runs no server of its own, and the developer can’t see any of them. Starting with version 2.0, the app also sends usage statistics to Google Analytics; the privacy policy has the details.
